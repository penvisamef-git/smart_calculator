Smart Calculator – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: your calculations stay on your phone. We do not have a server and we do not collect your calculations, names or history. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID.

This policy explains how the Smart Calculator app ("the app"), made by Cyber Tech System ("we"), handles information on Android and iOS.

1. Data stored only on your device

  • Calculations, their names, tags, pins and history.
  • Your settings: color theme, font, language, exchange rate, vibration and live result.
  • Whether you bought Premium, and how many free shares you used today.

This data never leaves your phone unless you choose to share it. You can delete one item or all history at any time in the app (History → Delete, or Settings → Delete all history). Uninstalling the app removes everything.

2. Microphone and voice input

If you tap the microphone button, the app asks for permission to use the microphone. Your voice is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service (Google on Android, Apple on iOS) so the app can read the numbers. We do not record, store or send your voice. Voice input only runs while you are using it.

3. Sharing

When you tap Share, the app opens your phone's share menu with the text of the calculations you selected. Only you choose where it goes (for example Telegram or Messenger).

4. Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and approximate location to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first. On iOS, Apple asks whether you allow tracking. You can reset or turn off personalised ads in your phone settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. In-app purchase

Premium ($0.99, one-time) is paid through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or payment details. The store only tells the app that the purchase was made, so Premium can be turned on and restored.

6. Home screen widget

The optional Android widget shows your last calculation on your home screen. The data stays on your phone.

7. Children

The app is a general calculator and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
